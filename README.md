# Halal Scroll: Quran & Sunnah

**Subtitle:** `Daily Ayah, Hadith & Tadabbur`

Replace mindless social media doom-scrolling with a serene, tactile full-screen vertical feed of the **Quran, Sahih Hadith, Prophetic Duas, and Seerah reflections**—paired with a 5-layer interactive **Deep Study** drawer and a studio-grade **Social Share Studio**.

## Core Features

- **Unified Islamic Feed & Semantic Grouping**: Blends connected Quran passages (e.g. *Surah Ash-Sharh 94:5–6*, *Surah Ad-Duha 93:1–5*, *Surah At-Talaq 65:2–3*), *Sahih* Hadiths (*Riyad as-Salihin*, *40 Hadith Nawawi*, *Al-Adab Al-Mufrad*), Prophetic Duas (*Hisn al-Muslim*), and Seerah moments.
- **3 Reading Modes**:
  1. **For You** (Balanced discovery feed)
  2. **Sequential** (Khatmah & book reader with saved card position)
  3. **Moods ("I'm Feeling...")** (Emotional state & life theme filters)
- **Synchronized Word-by-Word Illumination & Audio**: Real-time burnished gold (`#F5D061`) word highlighting during recitation and tappable Arabic word morphology popovers.
- **5-Layer Deep Study Drawer**:
  1. *Explain It Easier* (Plain-English Tadabbur summary + Key Takeaway)
  2. *Word-by-Word & Trilateral Root Explorer* (`ر ح م`, `ي س ر`, `ص ب ر`, etc. with Quranic occurrence counts)
  3. *Context & Classical Tafsir* (*Asbab al-Nuzul* + switcher for *Tafsir As-Sa'di*, *Ibn Kathir*, and *Ma'ariful Quran*)
  4. *Connected Sunnah* (Bidirectional Ayah ↔ Hadith cross-reference bridge)
  5. *Act On It Today* (Practical 1-minute Sunnah habit, private reflection journal & daily streak counter `🔥`)
- **Built-In Social Share Studio**:
  - Export **9:16**, **4:5**, and **1:1** Ultra-HD PNG cards, **4-Slide Deep Study Carousels**, and **Animated Video Reels / Audiograms** with synced word glow.

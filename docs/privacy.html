Privacy Policy & Canonical Sources

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Summary: Halal Scroll is designed with privacy first. We do not require an account, we do not sell personal data, and we do not display third-party behavioral ads.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

In the iOS, Android, and offline web versions of Halal Scroll, your reading preferences (theme, Arabic script style, translation edition, font size), bookmarks, custom folders, practiced reflections, and streak progress are stored locally on your device.

2. Network Requests & Audio Streaming

When you browse the full canonical feed online or stream Quranic audio recitations, the app fetches public, non-personal content from our servers and public recitation CDNs. Standard server logs (such as IP address and request timestamp) may be processed temporarily for security and reliability and are not used for tracking or profiling.

3. Children's Privacy

Halal Scroll is suitable for general audiences (4+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Canonical Sources & Attributions

  • Quranic Arabic Text: KFGQPC Uthmani & IndoPak scripts via Tanzil / Quranic corpus datasets.
  • English Translations: The Clear Quran (Dr. Mustafa Khattab) and Sahih International.
  • Hadith & Supplications: 40 Hadith Nawawi, Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, and Hisn al-Muslim.
  • Recitations: Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, and AbdulBaset AbdulSamad.

5. Contact

Questions, corrections, or privacy requests? Contact us via the Halal Scroll repository or support channel at halalscroll-production.up.railway.app.
